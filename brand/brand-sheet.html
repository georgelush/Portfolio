A / The job

A monogram for one engineer.

It stands for George Rusu, AI and automation engineer. It appears as a browser icon, a profile avatar, the corner of a CV and the first frame of a demo video.

It should feel technical and confident: something a person who keeps systems running would put their name behind. Ideas worth exploring: the letter G or the initials GR; a hint of a running process (a cursor, a step, a signal) without drawing a robot, a brain or a circuit.
