03 / Method

Three rules.

R1

Designed for the bad day

The happy path is the easy part. I design for the dropped network, the wrong input and the retry that arrives twice.

R2

A human approves what matters

Where money, customers or safety are involved, a person decides. Every decision leaves a trace.

R3

Tests and traces, not demos

If it cannot be tested, monitored and restored from backup, it is not finished.
